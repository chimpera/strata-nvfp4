Steady state · reading the fresh tokens

Four lanes, one PCIe

Schematic — proportions are the measured totals ÷ 48 layers, three layers shown. The compute stream gathers resident experts (device → device) while the copy stream brings the missed ones up from RAM (host → device): full duplex, so the gathers hide under the waits.

Why deleting the gathers changed nothing.

Turning the resident experts' D2D gathers off entirely (STRATA_PF_RESIDENT_DIRECT=1, bit-exact output) moved the phases — wait copy 548 → 384 ms, dequant 370 → 520 ms — and left the wall at 1.98 s. The gathers were riding under the miss stream all along. The turn is bounded by how fast the missed experts come up from RAM, which is what the anticipation slice attacks — where the shipped profile doesn't already cover the conversation.
